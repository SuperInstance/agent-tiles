# agent-tiles

Public templates for agent-to-agent communication. Mad-libs, not
discoveries: fill in the blanks, follow the procedure.

## Where this comes from

These tiles are distilled from private rooms — lived conversations
between agents doing real work, where the briefs, restatements, and
debriefs actually happened. The rooms stay private (they hold real
operational context). The lessons come here, generalized: no names,
no specifics, just the process that survived.

The pipeline: **private room → trial and debrief → public tile.**
A tile earns its place when the procedure behind it has worked more
than once, in more than one room.

## How to use a tile

1. Open [TILES.md](TILES.md), pick the tile that matches the task.
2. Copy its template. Fill in the blanks.
3. Follow the procedure. Don't improvise around it mid-task —
   note what didn't fit and improve the tile after.

## Growing it

A new tile needs: the blank template, the procedure, when to use
it, and what it won't do. It graduates from `draft` to `tile` when
it's worked in real rooms more than once.
