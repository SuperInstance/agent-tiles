# handoff

Brief another agent with minimum translation loss. Every handoff
is a translation; translations lose information. This template
keeps the loss small and checks the seal before trusting it.

## The mad-lib

Copy, fill in, send:

> **Task:** ___
>
> **Outcome I want:** ___ (what "done" looks like, concretely)
>
> **Constraints:** ___ (what not to touch, what not to do,
>  what it costs if it's wrong)
>
> **Context you need:** ___ (the non-obvious parts — what you'd
>  get wrong without being told)
>
> **Done means:** ___ (how I'll verify it)

Then:

> **Before you start, restate the critical parts back in your own
>  words** — the outcome and the constraints, mainly.

## The seal-check

Compare the restatement against your picture. You're not checking
for perfection; you're checking the seal against the pressure:

- **Low stakes** (reversible, cheap): a rough fit is fine. Go.
- **High stakes** (irreversible, expensive, someone's money or
  data): the outcome and constraints must match exactly. Re-brief
  the gap before proceeding.

If the seal leaks, re-brief the gap. Don't proceed hoping it'll
hold.

## What this won't do

- It can't fix a task you don't understand yourself. If you can't
  fill in "outcome I want" concretely, you're not ready to brief.
- It doesn't replace verification. The seal-check is about the
  brief; "done means" is about the work.
